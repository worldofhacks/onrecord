## Scope — read-only, deliberately

User connects their brokerage via SnapTrade's hosted portal (connectionType=
read; works across ~35 institutions INCLUDING Robinhood/Fidelity which are
read-only at the broker level anyway). OnRecord then shows THEIR holdings
crossed with the record: per held ticker — insider conduct, material events,
promises + outcome statuses in jurisdictions where the company appears,
mention-anchored performance. STRICTLY FACTUAL COPY (the T-033 pin, now
load-bearing): "you hold X; the record shows Y" — never a recommendation,
never a trade button, never push notifications (SnapTrade compliance policy
requires pull-based analysis; our own posture requires more).

## Pins
- No trade endpoints imported anywhere (grep-gated test, like T-057's verdict-word gate).
- userSecret stored encrypted server-side; never logged/echoed (T-014 secret discipline).
- Holdings sync respects SnapTrade's <=4 background calls/day/user; cached in the refresh-lane pattern.
- SnapTrade disclosure line + disconnect/delete affordance (their ToS requirement).
- Env-gated keyless 503 (ONRECORD_SNAPTRADE_CLIENT_ID/CONSUMER_KEY).

## Explicitly NOT this ticket
- Placing trades (see decision memo in this ticket's Context below) — a separate owner decision AFTER a securities-lawyer consult; capability-gated to Schwab/Webull-class brokers regardless (Robinhood/Fidelity cannot trade via any third party).
- Auto copy-trading: NOT BUILDABLE unregistered — FINRA 2025-07-29 alert defines it as the practice lawful only from registered advisers; SnapTrade's own policy reserves managed trading for RIAs. Off the table absent registration.
