## Context

The Promise Ledger holds 1,527 verbatim commitments (evalsets/promises.jsonl);
many contain hard quantities: megawatts, gallons/day, jobs, dollars (one-time
and annual). Extracting them into structured fields makes the tagline literal
and feeds T-057 outcome matching.

## Build sketch

- `onrecord/analysis/quantities.py`: deterministic pattern extraction over
  promise quote text (NOT over model output — the verbatim quote is the
  input, so extraction inherits the substring pin). Unit families:
  power (MW/megawatt/kW/GW), water (gallons/GPD/acre-feet), jobs
  (jobs/positions/employees), money ($X, "X million/billion dollars",
  annual vs one-time markers). Each extraction: {promise_id, kind, value,
  unit, raw_span} where raw_span is a VERBATIM substring of the quote —
  frozen tests pin the span-in-quote invariant plus a false-positive corpus
  ("2019", meeting ids, vote counts "5-0", times, addresses must not fire).
- Caption dysfluency handling: spoken numbers ("four hundred megawatts",
  "a million gallons a day") — deliberate scope decision: v1 extracts
  NUMERIC forms + a small spelled-number lexicon (units 1..100, million/
  billion multipliers); anything else stays unextracted (precision over
  recall — same posture as T-051's filter).
- Aggregates: per-jurisdiction and per-ticker rollups {promised_mw,
  promised_gpd, promised_jobs, promised_dollars_annual, promised_dollars_total,
  n_quantified}. Artifact: artifacts/promise_quantities.json (tracked;
  BOTH .gitignore and .dockerignore negations — LESSONS).
- API: /api/promises rows gain optional `quantities` field (additive);
  new GET /api/promised?by=jurisdiction|ticker serves rollups.
- UI: Ledger header strip "PROMISED ON THE RECORD: X MW · Y jobs · $Z"
  + per-jurisdiction table column. Both UI files cmp-identical.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: every raw_span is an exact substring of its promise quote (frozen test over the real promises artifact sample fixture).
- AC-2: false-positive corpus passes (dates, vote tallies, timestamps, ordinances numbers do not extract).
- AC-3: unit normalization pinned (kW->MW /1000, GW->MW *1000, "million gallons a day" -> GPD 1e6, money "million/billion" multipliers, annual vs total flagged).
- AC-4: aggregates reproducible from (promises.jsonl) alone; rebuild yields identical artifact.
- AC-5: /api/promises response stays backward-compatible (existing keys untouched); new endpoint keyless.
- AC-6: both UI files byte-identical; strip renders from pinned response shape.

## Out of scope

LLM-based quantity inference (v2 if precision floor unmet), promise sentiment/direction, currency other than USD.
