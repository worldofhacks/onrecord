## Context

Owner wants semantic headroom. The deployed store is `text-embedding-3-small`
@ 1536 (fp16 matrix 0.89 GB, NDCG@10 0.538 on the four-arm pool — current
best mode). Candidate: `text-embedding-3-large` @ 3072 (full dims, no
Matryoshka truncation), ~$9.50 re-embed over ~73.5M corpus tokens.

## Protocol (pooling-bias-aware — metrics.md Finding 2 applies)

1. **Build** `artifacts/embeddings-3large` with the existing
   `EmbeddingStore.embed_corpus` (checkpointed, resumable; identity
   chunking via `chunk_corpus`, same as deployed). No new production code —
   operational driver in scratchpad only.
2. **First read (biased)**: `python -m onrecord.rag.modes --store
   artifacts/embeddings-3large --history
   evalsets/modes-scoreboard-3large.jsonl` against the EXISTING judgment
   pool. Documented as a lower bound: 3-large's unique finds are unjudged
   and score 0 — the same mechanism that slandered semantic pre-repair.
3. **Fifth pooling arm**: pool 3-large-semantic's novel (query, doc) pairs
   over the 100 queries; label with gpt-5.6-sol under the session-3/4
   criterion-first protocol; provenance sidecar
   (`judgment-session-5-provenance.json`); append to
   `evalsets/judgments.jsonl`.
4. **Honest comparison**: re-score BOTH stores (all three modes each)
   against the augmented pool; both readings reported side by side. The
   augmented pool may move the baseline's numbers too — report old-pool and
   augmented-pool readings for both models; never compare across pools.

## Truncation census (2026-08-14, artifacts/embeddings-3large/truncation.json)

42 of 289,536 chunks (0.015%) exceed `EMBED_INPUT_MAX_CHARS=24000` and reach
the provider truncated — all EDGAR filing bodies/sections. Intersection with
the judgment set (2,246 rows, 100 queries):

- 10 judged rows sit on truncated docs (q66: 1, q74: 4, q75: 1, q82: 4)
- **2 are relevant-graded (grade ≥ 1), both in q82**:
  `edgar:MSFT:0001193125-26-323660:item7`,
  `edgar:ORCL:0001193125-26-277521:item7`

**Verdict: truncation cannot materially move NDCG@10.** Worst case it
perturbs one query's ranking out of 100 (≤0.01 on the mean, and only if
both docs' rankings flip on the truncated tail). Windowed chunking for long
filings remains worth doing for answer quality — filed to the corpus-v3
lane (T-053 runbook), not fixed here.

**Comparison fairness**: truncation is character-based
(`EMBED_INPUT_MAX_CHARS`) and model-independent — both stores embed
byte-identical inputs, so the 3-large vs 3-small comparison is unaffected
by it.

## Results (2026-08-14 — comparison COMPLETE)

Store built: 289,536 × 3072, 0 missing, 53.2 min wall (one transport-error
restart, checkpoint-resumed at 79,872 — nothing re-billed). Session 5:
1,390 novel pairs from the 3-large semantic top-20 arm (69.5% novelty),
gpt-5.6-sol, **1,390/1,390 graded, zero parse failures**; 53% of the novel
finds relevant (g1 337 + g2 400 vs g0 653). Pool: 2,246 → 3,636 rows.

| NDCG@10 | old pool (2,246) | augmented pool (3,636) |
|---|---|---|
| 3-small semantic | 0.5375 | 0.4614 |
| **3-large semantic** | 0.3298 (slandered) | **0.5505** |
| 3-small hybrid | 0.4309 | 0.4245 |
| **3-large hybrid** | 0.4011 | **0.4708** |
| lexical (store-independent) | 0.4489 | 0.4131 |

Same-pool deltas (the only valid comparison, AC-2): semantic **+0.089
(+19.3%)**, hybrid **+0.046 (+10.9%)**; semantic R@50 0.574 → 0.752, MRR
0.672 → 0.720, P@10 0.508 → 0.597. The old-pool reading of 3-large
(0.330) is the pooling-bias mechanism (metrics.md Finding 2) in its
purest form: judging the model's own arm swung its score by +0.22.
Lexical rows bit-identical per store (harness integrity check).

**Gate consequence (honest, surfaced not silenced)**: the augmented pool
moves the DEPLOYED store's best mode to 0.4614 — `make gate` now reads
FAIL for what ships. The 3-large store reads 0.5505 (PASS) in its sidecar
history. The gate stays red until the owner-gated swap deploys; do not
re-point the gate at the candidate store's history.

**Recommendation**: swap to 3-large @ 3072 in the corpus-v3 lane
(T-053 pin: post-demo-video, owner go). Railway RAM delta +0.9 GB fp16.
