## Context

ISO/RTO interconnection queues (MISO/PJM/ERCOT/CAISO/SPP) are structured,
public, keyless records of what was actually FILED with grid operators —
the counterpart to what was PROMISED in hearing rooms. BLOCKED-ON: the
deep-research agent's verification of exact URLs, formats, and county
fields; this ticket freezes scope only after that lands (its findings
amend this Context section before tests are written).

## Build sketch (to be confirmed against verified sources)

- `onrecord/ingest/iso_queues.py`: per-ISO fetcher (etiquette UA, retries,
  Retry-After, resumable), parser to a NORMALIZED row shape {iso, queue_id,
  county, state, mw, fuel/type, status, queue_date, withdrawn?}; DTD-reject
  posture for any XML; checksummed raw snapshots under artifacts/iso/.
- Jurisdiction join: county+state -> our 51 jurisdictions (explicit mapping
  table, misses logged not guessed).
- Cross view: per jurisdiction, promised MW (T-056) vs queued MW (this),
  as SEPARATE labeled columns — never summed, never presented as
  confirmation of each other (honesty pin, same posture as T-057).
- API: /api/grid?jurisdiction=...; UI: jurisdiction panel gains
  "IN THE QUEUE" strip beside "PROMISED ON THE RECORD".
- Corpus decision: queue rows are STRUCTURED data, not corpus docs — they
  do NOT enter the search index in v1 (no pooling-bias injection); they
  join at the UI/API layer.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1: parsers pinned by frozen fixtures captured from the real feeds (recorded in provenance sidecar with fetch date + checksum).

## Out of scope
